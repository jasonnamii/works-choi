# Master Term Sheet

Project: MAD POP / SAGONG EE HO Phase 1  
Date: May 27, 2026  
Document Status: Non-binding discussion draft, except for confidentiality, exclusivity, no-circumvention, governing law, dispute resolution, and costs if expressly agreed in definitive agreements.

## 1. Transaction Concept

The project shall be structured as a two-track agreement package under one master commercial framework.

```text
Agreement A:
Creative Director / Executive Producer / New IP Development Agreement

Agreement B:
SAGONG EE HO Limited IP License Agreement
```

Agreement A and Agreement B are interdependent project documents. Neither agreement shall become fully effective unless both agreements are duly executed, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 2. Parties

| Agreement | Parties | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement A | China Party and KHS Creative Company or Kim Hyung Seok | Creative direction, executive production, music supervision, new IP development |
| Agreement B | China Party and Noneuni | Limited license of SAGONG EE HO IP |
| Equity / Option Letter | China Party and KHS Creative Company or a designated equity holding entity | Strategic equity or option incentive |

Kim Hyung Seok shall be identified as the Key Creative Person for Agreement A.

Noneuni shall be identified as the rights holder or authorized licensor of SAGONG EE HO IP for Agreement B.

## 4. Agreement A Scope

Agreement A shall cover:

1. Creative Director role.
2. Executive Producer role.
3. New virtual artist and music IP development.
4. Music supervision.
5. China-localized creative package.
6. Review of core creative materials.
7. Reasonable participation in scheduled creative meetings.

Agreement A shall not cover:

1. Guarantee of SAGONG EE HO IP ownership.
2. Guarantee of China market revenue.
3. Guarantee of financing, listing, regulatory approval, or commercial success.
4. Guarantee of third-party artist participation.
5. Unlimited listing due diligence or audit cooperation.
6. Any personal guarantee by Kim Hyung Seok, unless separately agreed in writing.

## 6. No-Circumvention

The China Party shall not directly or indirectly bypass KHS Creative Company, Kim Hyung Seok, or Noneuni to obtain, develop, register, commercialize, sublicense, finance, tokenize, or exploit:

1. SAGONG EE HO;
2. any confusingly similar character, name, mark, domain, account, or digital avatar;
3. any derivative work based on SAGONG EE HO;
4. any new IP concept, music concept, character concept, world-building material, or creative material generated under the project.

## 7. Liability

Each party shall be liable only for its own agreement and its own scope.

KHS Creative Company shall not be liable for:

1. SAGONG EE HO ownership warranty;
2. regulatory failure;
3. financing failure;
4. listing delay;
5. market failure;
6. business interruption;
7. indirect damages;
8. expected profits;
9. losses caused by the China Party or its affiliates.

Noneuni shall not be liable for:

1. creative direction performance;
2. new IP development performance;
3. market performance;
4. listing, financing, regulatory, or operational results.

Aggregate liability shall be limited to direct damages caused by willful misconduct or gross negligence and shall not exceed the amount actually received by the relevant contracting party.

## 8. Cost and Recoupment Protection

Fees paid under Agreement A and Agreement B shall not be treated as:

1. shareholder loans;
2. project advances;
3. reimbursable costs;
4. operating expenses subject to recoupment;
5. listing expenses;
6. capital contribution refund obligations.

The China Party shall not demand refund, reimbursement, set-off, clawback, or recoupment of Agreement A fees or Agreement B fees based on project loss, financing delay, listing delay, regulatory delay, market failure, or operating cost overrun.

## 9. Equity / Option

Strategic equity or option may be granted to KHS Creative Company or a designated equity holding entity as long-term success incentive.

Such equity or option shall not be granted to Noneuni.

The equity or option shall be structured to allow sale, transfer, waiver, blind trust, or other required treatment if Korean public office, conflict-of-interest, tax, regulatory, or compliance requirements arise.
