# Agreement A Draft

Creative Director / Executive Producer / New IP Development Agreement  
Draft v1 - For Discussion Only  
Date: May 27, 2026

> This draft is a business and legal discussion draft. It is not a final legal document. Final review by Korean and Chinese counsel is required.

## 1. Parties

This Agreement is entered into by and between:

1. `[China Party]`, a company organized under the laws of `[jurisdiction]`, with its principal office at `[address]` (`China Party`); and
2. `[KHS Creative Company]`, a company designated and controlled by Kim Hyung Seok, with its principal office at `[address]` (`Creative Company`).

Kim Hyung Seok shall be the Key Creative Person under this Agreement.

If KHS Creative Company is not incorporated as of the signing date, this Agreement may be signed by Kim Hyung Seok or by an entity to be designated and controlled by Kim Hyung Seok.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to engage the Creative Company for:

1. creative direction;
2. executive production;
3. music supervision;
4. new IP development;
5. China-localized creative planning;
6. strategic review of the MAD POP Phase 1 project.

This Agreement does not transfer ownership of SAGONG EE HO IP or any Noneuni-owned IP.

## 3. Scope of Services

The Creative Company shall provide the following services:

1. Development of overall creative direction for the project.
2. Review of music, character, world-building, stage, and virtual artist concepts.
3. Development of new virtual artist and music IP concepts.
4. Music supervision for agreed project materials.
5. China-localized creative adaptation recommendations.
6. Participation in up to `[number]` scheduled creative meetings per month.
7. Delivery of a creative direction package, including concept notes, music direction, character direction, and launch recommendations.

Any service outside the above scope shall require a separate statement of work and separate fee.

## 4. Excluded Responsibilities

The Creative Company shall not be responsible for:

1. ownership warranty of SAGONG EE HO IP;
2. ownership warranty of any IP owned by Noneuni or third parties;
3. procurement or guarantee of third-party artists;
4. regulatory approval in China or any other jurisdiction;
5. financing, fundraising, listing, valuation, or capital market results;
6. revenue, profit, traffic, user acquisition, or market performance;
7. operation of the China Party's business;
8. legal, tax, accounting, compliance, or licensing filings of the China Party;
9. project losses, fines, penalties, business suspension losses, or listing delays not caused by willful misconduct or gross negligence of the Creative Company.

## 5. Fee

The China Party shall pay the Creative Company a total fee of KRW `[4.5 billion to 4.7 billion]`.

The fee shall be paid as follows:

| Milestone | Payment |
|---|---:|
| Signing of this Agreement | 40% |
| Delivery of Creative Direction Package | 30% |
| China Localization / New IP Development Milestone | 20% |
| Launch Readiness Review | 10% |

All fees are non-refundable except in case of fraud or willful non-performance finally determined by arbitration.

Fees under this Agreement shall not be treated as shareholder loans, reimbursable costs, advances, project expenses, listing expenses, or amounts subject to recoupment or clawback.

## 6. Equity or Option

As long-term success incentive for Kim Hyung Seok's creative contribution, the China Party shall grant the Creative Company, or its designated equity holding entity, a 30% strategic equity interest or 30% equity option in `[project company / holding company]`.

Such equity or option shall:

1. not be granted to Noneuni;
2. not impose personal liability on Kim Hyung Seok;
3. be transferable, sellable, waivable, cash-settleable, or trustable if required by Korean public office, conflict-of-interest, tax, foreign exchange, regulatory, or compliance requirements;
4. be documented in a separate equity or option agreement.

## 7. Intellectual Property

All pre-existing IP of the Creative Company and Kim Hyung Seok shall remain owned by the relevant original owner.

New IP concepts developed under this Agreement shall be treated as follows:

1. broad concepts, music direction, creative direction, and development materials remain owned by the Creative Company unless expressly assigned;
2. the China Party receives a limited project-use license only within the agreed project scope;
3. commercial exploitation outside the project requires a separate written agreement;
4. no AI training, dataset creation, model training, tokenization, securitization, or sublicense is permitted without separate written consent.

## 8. Key Person

Kim Hyung Seok is the Key Creative Person.

If Kim Hyung Seok is temporarily unavailable due to health, schedule, force majeure, public duty, or other reasonable cause, the Creative Company may provide substitute support through its designated team, provided that final creative direction remains under Kim Hyung Seok's reasonable supervision when available.

No personal guarantee, unlimited liability, or personal indemnity shall be imposed on Kim Hyung Seok.

## 9. Liability

The Creative Company shall be liable only for direct damages caused by its willful misconduct or gross negligence.

The Creative Company shall not be liable for:

1. indirect damages;
2. consequential damages;
3. expected profits;
4. project losses;
5. business interruption;
6. fines or penalties not directly caused by the Creative Company;
7. financing failure;
8. listing delay;
9. regulatory failure;
10. market failure.

The aggregate liability of the Creative Company shall not exceed the amount actually received by the Creative Company under this Agreement.

## 10. Cooperation for Financing or Listing

The Creative Company shall provide reasonable cooperation for financing or listing materials only to the extent:

1. directly related to the services under this Agreement;
2. requested with reasonable prior written notice;
3. not requiring false, misleading, or unverified statements;
4. not imposing additional cost unless reimbursed by the China Party;
5. not requiring disclosure of confidential personal, tax, legal, or third-party information beyond reasonable scope.

The Creative Company shall not be liable for financing failure, listing failure, listing delay, valuation failure, or regulatory inquiry outcomes.

## 11. No-Circumvention

The China Party shall not bypass the Creative Company or Kim Hyung Seok to obtain, register, commercialize, sublicense, finance, tokenize, or exploit new IP concepts, music concepts, character concepts, world-building materials, or creative materials generated under this Agreement.

## 12. Termination

The Creative Company may suspend performance or terminate this Agreement if:

1. payment is delayed by more than `[10]` business days;
2. the China Party uses materials outside the agreed scope;
3. the China Party attempts unauthorized registration, sublicense, AI training, or tokenization;
4. the China Party materially breaches confidentiality or no-circumvention obligations;
5. performance becomes illegal or commercially unreasonable due to regulatory issues.

Upon termination, all unpaid fees for completed milestones shall become immediately due.
