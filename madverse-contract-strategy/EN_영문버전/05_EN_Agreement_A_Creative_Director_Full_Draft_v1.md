## 2. Background

2.1 The China Party is developing the MAD POP project, including AI music, virtual artist, digital human concert, music social community, IP expansion, and related commercial activities.

2.2 The China Party has requested the creative participation of KHS as a leading Korean music producer and creative figure.

2.3 The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement covers creative direction, executive production, music supervision, and new IP development services only.

2.4 The Parties further acknowledge that SAGONG EE HO IP is owned or controlled separately by Noneuni and shall be licensed under a separate SAGONG EE HO Limited IP License Agreement.

2.5 This Agreement and the SAGONG EE HO Limited IP License Agreement are linked project documents, but each party shall be liable only for its own contract scope.

## 4. Scope of Services

4.1 The Creative Company shall provide the following Creative Services:

1. overall creative direction for the Project;
2. executive production review for the Project's music, virtual artist, stage, and character concept;
3. music supervision and direction;
4. new virtual artist and music IP development;
5. China-localized creative planning;
6. review of approved creative materials;
7. up to `[number]` scheduled creative meetings per month;
8. one creative direction package containing project concept, music direction, character direction, world-building direction, and launch recommendations;
9. reasonable written comments on project materials submitted by the China Party.

4.2 Any additional services shall require a separate written statement of work and separate fee.

4.3 The Creative Company shall not be required to provide unlimited meetings, unlimited revisions, artist procurement, full-time employment, legal compliance, financing support, listing support, or third-party guarantee unless separately agreed in writing.

## 6. Fee and Payment

6.1 The China Party shall pay the Creative Company a total creative package fee of KRW `[4,500,000,000 to 4,700,000,000]` (`Creative Fee`).

6.2 The Creative Fee shall be paid as follows:

| Milestone | Payment |
|---|---:|
| Execution of this Agreement | 40% |
| Delivery of Creative Direction Package | 30% |
| China Localization / New IP Development Milestone | 20% |
| Launch Readiness Review | 10% |

6.3 Each payment shall be made within `[5]` business days after the applicable milestone.

6.4 All bank charges, remittance costs, withholding, VAT, and tax filing matters shall be handled as set out in Article 7.

6.5 The Creative Fee is non-refundable except in case of fraud or willful non-performance finally determined by arbitration.

6.6 The Creative Fee shall not be treated as:

1. shareholder loan;
2. project advance;
3. reimbursable cost;
4. operating expense subject to recoupment;
5. listing expense;
6. capital contribution refund;
7. amount subject to set-off, clawback, or repayment due to project loss, listing delay, financing delay, regulatory delay, or market failure.

## 7. Taxes and Remittance

7.1 The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to determine the appropriate tax treatment of the Creative Fee.

7.2 Any withholding tax required by applicable law shall be withheld and remitted by the paying party, and the paying party shall provide official tax payment certificates and supporting documents.

7.3 The Parties shall discuss whether the Creative Fee is treated as service fee, consulting fee, creative production fee, or other appropriate category under applicable tax law.

7.4 The China Party shall not re-characterize the Creative Fee as royalty, equity contribution, loan, cost reimbursement, or recoupable advance without the Creative Company's prior written consent.

7.5 Each Party shall be responsible for its own income tax unless otherwise agreed.

## 9. Intellectual Property

9.1 All pre-existing IP of KHS, the Creative Company, and their affiliates shall remain owned by the original owner.

9.2 SAGONG EE HO IP shall not be licensed or transferred under this Agreement.

9.3 Subject to full payment of the Creative Fee, the China Party shall receive a limited, project-specific, non-transferable license to use approved Creative Materials only for the Project in the Territory during the Term.

9.4 The China Party shall not:

1. register Creative Materials as its own IP;
2. use Creative Materials outside the Project;
3. use Creative Materials for AI training or dataset creation;
4. tokenize, securitize, pledge, or asset-back Creative Materials;
5. sublicense Creative Materials;
6. create replacement or confusingly similar IP to bypass the Creative Company, KHS, or Noneuni.

9.5 Ownership of New IP shall be agreed separately in writing before commercialization. Unless separately agreed, the China Party shall only have a limited project-use license.

## 15. Term and Termination

15.1 This Agreement shall commence on the execution date and continue for `[3]` years unless terminated earlier.

15.2 The Creative Company may suspend performance or terminate this Agreement if:

1. payment is delayed by more than `[10]` business days;
2. the China Party uses Creative Materials outside the approved scope;
3. the China Party attempts unauthorized registration, sublicense, AI training, tokenization, or securitization;
4. the China Party breaches confidentiality or no-circumvention obligations;
5. the China Party materially breaches the Linked IP Agreement;
6. performance becomes illegal or commercially unreasonable due to regulatory issues.

15.3 The China Party may terminate this Agreement if the Creative Company commits material breach and fails to cure within `[30]` days after written notice.

15.4 Upon termination, all unpaid fees for completed milestones shall become due immediately.

---

## 16. Effect of Termination

Upon expiration or termination:

1. the China Party shall cease use of unpaid or unauthorized Creative Materials;
2. the China Party shall not use Creative Materials outside the paid and approved scope;
3. confidentiality, no-circumvention, IP, unpaid fee, liability limit, and dispute clauses shall survive;
4. termination shall not require refund of properly earned and paid Creative Fees.

---

17.1 The Creative Company shall be liable only for direct damages caused by willful misconduct or gross negligence.

17.2 The Creative Company shall not be liable for:

1. indirect damages;
2. consequential damages;
3. expected profits;
4. project losses;
5. business interruption;
6. regulatory fines not directly caused by the Creative Company;
7. financing failure;
8. listing delay;
9. market failure;
