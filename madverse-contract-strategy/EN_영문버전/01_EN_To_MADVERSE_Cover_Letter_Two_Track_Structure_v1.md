# Cover Letter to MADVERSE

Date: May 27, 2026  
Subject: Proposal to Restructure the JV Draft into a Two-Track Project Agreement

Dear MADVERSE Team,

Thank you for sharing the Joint Venture Agreement draft for the MAD POP project.

We fully understand the commercial intention behind the draft. The draft reflects a broad platform vision, including AI music, virtual artists, digital human concerts, social music communities, IP expansion, and long-term capital market potential.

After reviewing the draft, we believe the project can be executed more clearly and efficiently if the structure is reorganized into a two-track agreement package.

The reason is simple. The project is based on two distinct assets:

1. Kim Hyung Seok's creative direction, executive production, music supervision, and new IP development capability.
2. Noneuni's ownership and limited licensing of the SAGONG EE HO IP.

These two assets are connected to the same project, but they have different legal owners, different commercial functions, and different liability profiles. For clarity of IP ownership, payment, tax, accounting, liability, and project execution, we propose to restructure the current JV draft into two linked agreements under one master term sheet.

## Proposed Structure

```text
Master Term Sheet
 ├─ Agreement A: Creative Director / Executive Producer / New IP Development Agreement
 │  Parties: China Party and KHS Creative Company or Kim Hyung Seok
 │
 └─ Agreement B: SAGONG EE HO Limited IP License Agreement
    Parties: China Party and Noneuni
```

This structure is not intended to reduce commitment. It is intended to make execution faster, cleaner, and more legally accurate.

Under this structure:

- Kim Hyung Seok remains the Key Creative Person of the project.
- The creative direction, executive production, music supervision, and new IP development are handled under Agreement A.
- SAGONG EE HO IP is licensed separately by its rights holder under Agreement B.
- The two agreements are linked as one project package.
- Each contracting party is responsible only for its own scope.
- Equity or option incentives may be discussed separately as a long-term success alignment mechanism.

## Key Business Point

The value of this project does not come from a single IP asset alone. It comes from the combination of:

1. Kim Hyung Seok's creative authority and development capability; and
2. SAGONG EE HO as the first limited IP asset for Phase 1.

Therefore, a two-track structure is more accurate than putting all assets, obligations, and liabilities into one broad JV document.

## Next Step

We have prepared a Master Term Sheet that summarizes the proposed structure, key commercial terms, IP scope, payment structure, liability limits, and dispute framework.

We suggest using the Master Term Sheet as the basis for the next discussion, and then preparing the definitive agreements after both sides align on the structure.

We look forward to discussing this structure with you.

Sincerely,

Korean Project Team
