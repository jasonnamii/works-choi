> This draft is a business and legal discussion draft. It is not a final legal document. Final review by Korean and Chinese counsel is required.

## 1. Parties

This Agreement is entered into by and between:

1. `[China Party]`, a company organized under the laws of `[jurisdiction]`, with its principal office at `[address]` (`Licensee`); and
2. `Noneuni`, a company organized under the laws of the Republic of Korea, with its principal office at `[address]` (`Licensor`).

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to grant the Licensee a limited license to use SAGONG EE HO IP for MAD POP Phase 1 in Mainland China.

This Agreement does not transfer ownership of SAGONG EE HO IP.

This Agreement is linked to the Creative Director / Executive Producer / New IP Development Agreement between the China Party and KHS Creative Company or Kim Hyung Seok.

## 3. Licensed IP

The Licensed IP means only the following approved materials:

1. SAGONG EE HO character name;
2. approved character images and visual identity;
3. approved world-building and story reference materials;
4. approved style guide;
5. approved reference assets delivered by Licensor.

The Licensed IP does not include:

1. ownership of SAGONG EE HO;
2. source files not expressly delivered;
3. trademarks not expressly licensed;
4. music, sound recordings, artist rights, portrait rights, personality rights, or third-party materials;
5. new IP, new characters, new songs, or creative concepts developed by Kim Hyung Seok or KHS Creative Company unless separately agreed.

## 4. License Grant

Licensor grants Licensee a limited, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, project-specific commercial license to use the Licensed IP only within:

| Item | Scope |
|---|---|
| Territory | Mainland China |
| Project | MAD POP Phase 1 |
| Term | 3 years |
| Renewal | 2-year renewal option, subject to payment, compliance, and written approval |
| Media | Approved project materials only |
| Exclusivity | Limited exclusivity within the territory, term, and project scope only |

Any use outside the above scope requires prior written approval from Licensor.

## 5. License Fee and Sales-Based Running Royalty

Licensee shall pay Licensor an upfront / minimum guarantee license fee of KRW `[300 million to 500 million]`.

License fee payment schedule:

| Milestone | Payment |
|---|---:|
| Signing | 70% |
| Delivery of approved IP asset package | 30% |

In addition to the license fee, Licensee shall pay the following sales-based running royalty:

| Revenue Source | Royalty |
|---|---:|
| content, service, or project revenue directly using Licensed IP | 3% of Net Sales |
| merchandise, character goods, or commercialization revenue | 5% of Net Sales |
| sublicense or relicensing receipts | 25% of Net Receipts |

`Net Sales` means gross receipts less VAT, refunds, and approved payment processing fees only. Marketing costs, labor costs, internal operating costs, affiliate fees, platform development costs, and listing costs shall not be deducted.

The license fee and running royalty are separate from:

1. project investment;
2. operating costs;
3. shareholder loans;
4. recoupable advances;
5. listing costs;
6. recoupment or clawback.

The license fee and properly earned running royalty are non-refundable except in case of fraud or willful non-delivery finally determined by arbitration.

## 6. Restrictions

Licensee shall not:

1. sublicense the Licensed IP;
2. transfer this Agreement;
3. use the Licensed IP outside Mainland China;
4. use the Licensed IP outside MAD POP Phase 1;
5. register SAGONG EE HO or confusingly similar names, marks, accounts, domains, characters, or digital avatars;
6. create derivative works without approval;
7. use the Licensed IP for AI model training, dataset creation, synthetic content generation, or machine learning without separate written consent;
8. tokenize, securitize, pledge, finance, or asset-back the Licensed IP;
9. use the Licensed IP in NFTs, digital collectibles, digital asset trading, IP asset-backed securitization, or exchange products without separate written approval;
10. alter the core character identity without approval;
11. use the Licensed IP after expiration or termination.

## 7. Approval Rights

Licensee shall obtain Licensor's prior written approval for:

1. character design changes;
2. derivative works;
3. virtual idol adaptation;
4. merchandising;
5. advertising campaign use;
6. live performance use;
7. AI-generated versions;
8. public launch materials;
9. trademark, copyright, or platform account filings;
10. sublicense or co-branding proposals.

Licensor shall review materials within a commercially reasonable period after receipt of complete materials.

## 8. Quality Control

Licensee shall maintain the quality, reputation, and integrity of SAGONG EE HO.

Licensee shall not use the Licensed IP in any manner that:

1. damages the reputation of SAGONG EE HO, Noneuni, Kim Hyung Seok, or related Korean project parties;
2. violates applicable law;
3. creates misleading ownership or endorsement claims;
4. conflicts with approved creative direction;
5. exposes the Licensed IP to regulatory, political, obscene, defamatory, or discriminatory content risk.

## 9. Ownership

All rights, title, and interest in and to SAGONG EE HO IP shall remain with Licensor.

No ownership, assignment, pledge, security interest, permanent license, irrevocable license, or perpetual license is granted under this Agreement.

All goodwill arising from use of the Licensed IP shall inure to the benefit of Licensor.

## 10. Derivative Works

Derivative works may be created only with prior written approval.

Unless otherwise agreed:

1. SAGONG EE HO-based derivative elements shall be owned by Licensor;
2. Licensee may use approved derivative works only during the term and within the approved project scope;
3. no derivative work may be registered by Licensee without Licensor's written approval;
4. derivative works shall not be used to create a competing or replacement IP.

## 11. No-Circumvention

Licensee shall not bypass Licensor, KHS Creative Company, or Kim Hyung Seok to obtain, register, develop, commercialize, or exploit:

1. SAGONG EE HO;
2. confusingly similar IP;
3. derivative characters;
4. replacement characters based on SAGONG EE HO;
5. new IP concepts created under the linked project documents.

## 12. Termination

Licensor may immediately suspend or terminate this Agreement if:

1. Licensee fails to pay;
2. Licensee uses the Licensed IP outside the approved scope;
3. Licensee sublicenses without approval;
4. Licensee registers or attempts to register Licensed IP or confusingly similar IP;
5. Licensee uses the Licensed IP for AI training without consent;
6. Licensee creates unauthorized derivatives;
7. Licensee breaches quality control obligations;
8. Licensee becomes insolvent, liquidated, or ceases the project;
9. the linked Agreement A is not executed or is terminated for material breach by the China Party.

## 13. Effect of Termination

Upon expiration or termination, Licensee shall:

1. immediately cease all use of the Licensed IP;
2. remove public materials;
3. stop production and distribution;
4. return or destroy confidential and IP materials;
5. transfer or close project accounts using SAGONG EE HO;
6. certify compliance in writing;
7. not use derivative works based on the Licensed IP.

## 14. Liability

Licensor shall be liable only for direct damages caused by willful misconduct or gross negligence.

Licensor shall not be liable for:

1. market performance;
2. China regulatory approval;
3. financing;
4. listing;
5. project operation;
6. creative direction performance;
7. third-party artist participation;
8. indirect damages;
9. expected profits;
10. business interruption.

The aggregate liability of Licensor shall not exceed the license fee actually received by Licensor.
