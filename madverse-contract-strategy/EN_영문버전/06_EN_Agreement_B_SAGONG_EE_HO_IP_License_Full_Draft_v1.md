## 5. License Grant

5.1 Subject to full payment and compliance, Licensor grants Licensee a conditional long-term exclusive use right to use the Licensed IP only within the scope below. This structure is intended to provide Licensee with the rights stability required for long-term operation, investment recovery, and listing plans.

| Item | Scope |
|---|---|
| Territory | global excluding Korea |
| Project | MAD POP and projects agreed in writing by the parties |
| Term | initial 5 years |
| Renewal | automatic renewal in 5-year periods subject to minimum guarantee payment, launch and revenue conditions, brand guideline compliance, accounting report delivery, and absence of material breach |
| Maximum Term | 15 years as the base position; up to 20 years may be discussed if required for Licensee's listing stability |
| Media | Approved project materials only |
| Exclusivity | conditional exclusivity within Territory, Term, and Project only |

5.2 No right is granted outside the Territory, Term, Project, and Approved Materials.

5.3 Korean rights, use outside MAD POP and agreed projects, and any new business, derivative work, AI training, or tokenization use without separate approval are excluded from this license.

## 6. License Fee and Sales-Based Running Royalty

6.1 Licensee shall pay Licensor an upfront / minimum guarantee license fee of KRW `[300,000,000 to 500,000,000]` (`License Fee`).

6.2 License Fee payment schedule:

| Milestone | Payment |
|---|---:|
| Execution of this Agreement | 70% |
| Delivery of approved IP asset package | 30% |

6.3 In addition to the License Fee, Licensee shall pay Licensor the following sales-based Running Royalty.

| Revenue Source | Royalty |
|---|---:|
| content, service, or project revenue directly using Licensed IP | 3% of Net Sales |
| merchandise, character goods, or commercialization revenue | 5% of Net Sales |
| sublicense or relicensing receipts | 25% of Net Receipts |

6.4 Running Royalty shall be calculated and paid within `[30]` days after the end of each calendar quarter.

6.5 Licensee shall provide Licensor with quarterly sales reports, usage reports, sublicense receipt details, and related accounting records.

6.6 Licensor may audit the records used to calculate Running Royalty once per year upon reasonable prior notice. If the audit reveals an underpayment exceeding 5% of the amount paid for the relevant period, Licensee shall bear the audit cost.

6.7 The License Fee and properly earned Running Royalty are non-refundable except in case of fraud or willful non-delivery finally determined by arbitration.

6.8 The License Fee and Running Royalty shall not be treated as:

1. project investment;
2. operating cost;
3. shareholder loan;
4. recoupable advance;
5. listing cost;
6. amount subject to recoupment, clawback, refund, or set-off.

## 17. Term and Termination

17.1 This Agreement shall commence on the execution date and continue for an initial term of 5 years.

17.2 Licensee may automatically renew this Agreement in 5-year periods if all of the following conditions are satisfied. The total term shall be 15 years as the base position, and may be extended up to 20 years by written agreement if required for Licensee's listing stability.

1. all minimum guarantee and due payment amounts have been paid;
2. MAD POP or an agreed project has been substantially launched in the global market excluding Korea;
3. agreed revenue, operation, or financing conditions have been satisfied;
4. brand guidelines and quality control standards have been complied with;
5. accounting reports and usage reports have been delivered;
6. no unauthorized derivative work, AI training, sublicensing, rights registration attempt, or brand damage has occurred;
7. no unauthorized transfer of Licensee's equity, operating control, or project rights has occurred;
8. no material breach has occurred.

17.3 Licensor may immediately suspend or terminate this Agreement if:

1. Licensee fails to pay;
2. Licensee uses Licensed IP outside approved scope;
3. Licensee sublicenses without approval;
4. Licensee registers or attempts to register Licensed IP or confusingly similar IP;
5. Licensee uses Licensed IP for AI training without consent;
6. Licensee creates unauthorized Derivative Works;
7. Licensee breaches quality control obligations;
8. Licensee fails to deliver accounting reports or usage reports;
9. Licensee transfers equity, operating control, or project rights without authorization;
10. Licensee becomes insolvent, liquidated, or ceases the Project;
11. the Linked Creative Agreement is not executed or is terminated due to material breach by the China Party;
12. performance becomes illegal or commercially unreasonable due to regulatory changes.

17.4 Licensee may terminate if Licensor commits material breach and fails to cure within `[30]` days after written notice.

## 18. Effect of Termination

Upon expiration or termination, Licensee shall:

1. immediately cease all use of Licensed IP;
2. remove public materials;
3. stop production and distribution;
4. return or destroy confidential and IP materials;
5. transfer or close accounts using SAGONG EE HO;
6. certify compliance in writing;
7. stop using Derivative Works;
8. not claim refund of properly earned and paid License Fees or Running Royalties.
